01 Market sizing

Hidradenitis suppurativa market opportunity

A Bayesian estimate of the treated-market opportunity under incomplete information.

Case details

QuestionHow large is the commercially addressable population once diagnosis, treatment and access constraints are considered?

ApproachCombined standardized epidemiology, Mexico’s age-sex structure, diagnostic delay, severity and explicit access priors in a reproducible Bayesian workflow.

DecisionEstimated a P50 of 5,204 persistent public-sector patient-years and made the principal access uncertainties visible.

08 Hospital analytics

Hospital expenditure and case-mix benchmarking

An MVP design linking exercised budgets, therapeutic areas and treatment routes to relative resource intensity.

MVP methodology Case details

QuestionWhich combinations of scale, diagnosis and treatment pathway explain hospital expenditure, and which institutions differ from comparable providers?

ApproachBuild a hospital-year panel, discover stable procedure bundles within 15–20 therapeutic areas and apply partially penalized LASSO only to diagnosis–route interactions.

Expected outputRelative resource-intensity indices, expected expenditure and an observed-to-expected benchmark with explicit interpretation limits.
